I am a Research Economist at the Bank of Portugal and an Adjunct Assistant Professor at Católica Lisbon School of Business & Economics. I am also a CESifo Research Affiliate, an Academic Visitor at the University of Oxford and a member of the CUBE research unit at Católica Lisbon. My primary research interests are in international economics and macro-development.
